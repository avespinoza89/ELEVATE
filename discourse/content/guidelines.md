# ELEVATE Commons Community Guidelines

## Welcome to Our Community

ELEVATE Commons is built on trust, respect, and a shared commitment to transforming place-based philanthropy. These guidelines help us create a space where everyone can learn, collaborate, and elevate their impact.

---

## Our Core Values

### 🤝 Respect
Treat all community members with courtesy, professionalism, and respect – even when you disagree.

### 📚 Learning
Embrace a learning mindset. We're all here to grow and improve our practice.

### 🌍 Inclusion
Welcome diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and experiences. Everyone's voice matters.

### 💡 Collaboration
Work together, share generously, and support each other's success.

### 🎯 Purpose
Keep discussions focused on our shared mission: transforming place-based philanthropy.

---

## Expected Behavior

### ✅ DO:

#### Be Respectful & Professional
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what's best for the community

#### Contribute Meaningfully
- Share your knowledge and experiences generously
- Ask thoughtful questions
- Provide context for your posts
- Stay on topic
- Search before posting to avoid duplicates

#### Give Credit
- Cite sources for research and data
- Acknowledge others' contributions
- Request permission before sharing others' work
- Respect intellectual property

#### Protect Privacy
- Don't share others' personal information
- Respect confidentiality when appropriate
- Ask before sharing details about specific communities or initiatives
- Be mindful of what you share publicly

#### Assume Good Intent
- Give others the benefit of the doubt
- Ask clarifying questions rather than assuming
- Approach disagreements with curiosity
- Seek to understand before being understood

#### Build Community
- Welcome new members
- Help others find resources
- Participate in discussions
- Share successes and lessons learned

---

## Prohibited Behavior

### ❌ DON'T:

#### Harassment & Discrimination
- Personal attacks or insults
- Discriminatory language or behavior
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment

#### Disruptive Behavior
- Spam or excessive self-promotion
- Off-topic posts or thread derailment
- Posting the same content repeatedly
- Deliberately inflammatory posts ("trolling")
- Sockpuppeting (using multiple accounts)

#### Inappropriate Content
- Profanity or explicit content
- Political or partisan content unrelated to philanthropy
- Solicitations or advertising (without moderator approval)
- Copyrighted material without permission
- Confidential or proprietary information

#### Dishonesty
- Impersonating others
- Plagiarism
- Spreading misinformation
- Misrepresenting credentials or affiliation

## Specific Guidelines by Category

### General Discussion
- Keep it relevant to place-based philanthropy

- Be welcoming to newcomers

- Cite your sources
- Distinguish between evidence-based practices and opinions

- Be punctual
- Participate actively
- Respect presenters and other attendees

- Share lessons learned

- Respect community confidentiality

- Search existing topics first
- Provide context for your question

- Pay it forward by answering others' questions

## Content Standards

### Quality Over Quantity
- Post well-thought-out contributions
- Take time to craft clear, concise posts
- Edit for clarity and grammar
- Break up long posts with headings and formatting

### Constructive Disagreement
When you disagree:
1. Acknowledge points of agreement
2. Explain your perspective respectfully
3. Support your position with evidence
4. Seek common ground
5. Know when to agree to disagree

### Sources & Citations
When sharing research or data:
- Cite your sources
- Link to original research when possible
- Distinguish between published and unpublished work
- Note any conflicts of interest

### Confidentiality
Before sharing information about:
- Specific grantees or communities
- Internal foundation processes
- Unpublished research
- Ongoing initiatives

Ask yourself: "Do I have permission to share this publicly?"

## Community Participation

### Trust Levels
ELEVATE Commons uses a trust level system:

**Trust Level 0** (New User)
- Read topics
- Reply to topics
- Create topics (with limits)

**Trust Level 1** (Basic User)
- All Level 0 abilities
- Upload images
- Use all post formatting
- Daily posting limits increase

**Trust Level 2** (Member)
- All Level 1 abilities
- Access to Member Lounge
- Longer posts
- More uploads
- Can recategorize topics

**Trust Level 3** (Regular)
- All Level 2 abilities
- Can edit all posts
- Can split and merge topics
- Access to all categories
- Higher upload limits

**Trust Level 4** (Leader/Moderator)
- All Level 3 abilities
- Can edit others' posts
- Can pin/unpin topics
- Can close topics
- Moderation tools

### Earning Trust
Trust levels are earned through:
- Regular participation
- Quality contributions
- Time in community
- Giving and receiving likes
- Reading posts
- Visiting regularly

## Moderation

### Our Approach
We practice **gentle moderation** that:
- Assumes good intent
- Educates rather than punishes
- Escalates only when necessary
- Supports community self-governance

### What Moderators Do
Moderators:
- Welcome new members
- Answer questions
- Move off-topic posts
- Edit for clarity or formatting (with note)
- Remind members of guidelines
- Address violations
- Facilitate discussions

### Reporting Issues
If you see something concerning:
1. **Flag it**: Use the flag function (🚩) to report to moderators
2. **Explain**: Provide context about the issue
3. **Trust the process**: Moderators will review and respond

Don't:
- Publicly call out members
- Respond to trolls or inflammatory posts
- Escalate conflicts

### Moderation Actions
Based on severity, moderators may:

**Level 1: Gentle Reminder**
- Private message with guidelines
- Public reminder (if pattern develops)
- Edit post with explanation

**Level 2: Warning**
- Official warning via private message
- Temporary suspension (1-7 days)
- Required acknowledgment of guidelines

**Level 3: Serious Violation**
- Extended suspension (1-30 days)
- Removal from specific categories
- Required meeting with moderator

**Level 4: Permanent Ban**
- Reserved for severe or repeated violations
- Includes IP ban
- Appeal process available

### Appeals
If you receive a moderation action you believe is unfair:
1. Email hello@unitelearnelevate.com
2. Explain your perspective
3. Allow 3-5 business days for review
4. Accept the final decision

## Special Situations

### Sensitive Topics
When discussing sensitive issues:
- Be extra thoughtful about language
- Acknowledge different perspectives
- Focus on philanthropy implications
- Avoid partisan politics

### Conflicts of Interest
Disclose when:
- You have financial interest in something you're sharing
- You work for an organization being discussed
- You're sharing your own organization's resources
- You have personal relationships relevant to the topic

### Research & Data
When sharing:
- Distinguish between published and unpublished
- Note any limitations or caveats
- Respect embargo dates
- Honor data sharing agreements

### Photography & Media
When sharing images or videos:
- Have permission from people in photos
- Don't share images that could identify vulnerable populations
- Credit photographers
- Note if you're sharing from public sources

## The Spirit of the Guidelines

While we've outlined specific rules and expectations, the spirit of these guidelines is simple:

**Be kind. Be professional. Be curious. Be collaborative.**

We trust our community members to use good judgment and act in the best interests of the community. When in doubt, ask yourself:

- Does this contribute to our mission?
- Would I say this in a professional setting?
- Am I treating others as I'd want to be treated?
- Is this building up or tearing down our community?
