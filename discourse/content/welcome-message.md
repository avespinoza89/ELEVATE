# Welcome to ELEVATE Commons! 🎉

## Unite. Learn. ELEVATE

We're thrilled to have you join our community of place-based funders dedicated to transforming philanthropy through learning, collaboration, and innovation.

### What is ELEVATE?

ELEVATE Commons is a dedicated space for place-based funders, particularly those focused on learning and evaluation, to:

- **Unite** with fellow changemakers working to elevate communities
- **Learn** from shared experiences, research, and best practices
- **ELEVATE** our collective impact through collaboration and innovation

### Our Mission

Transforming Place-Based Philanthropy by breaking down silos, sharing lessons from past efforts, and forging partnerships that transcend geographical boundaries.

### How We Work Together

🤝 **Take Action**: Co-design tools and strategies that redefine effective place-based philanthropy

📚 **Learn Collaboratively**: Discuss successes and setbacks, tap the hivemind to tackle tough challenges

🔗 **Share Resources**: Access curated tools, templates, research, and best practices

🌐 **Expand Your Network**: Forge meaningful connections with fellow changemakers

### Our Core Principles

**1. Transformational, not transactional**
We view data and evaluation not as compliance tasks, but as catalysts for transformative learning and growth.

**2. Community at the center**
We prioritize community expertise and collaborate in ways that are inclusive, non-extractive, and reduce burden on grant partners.

**3. Bringing our whole selves**
We believe the best work happens when people can bring their whole selves and have fun.

**4. Innovative progress**
We push boundaries and advance place-based funding to make a real difference.

### Getting Started

**New members, we recommend you:**

1. **Introduce yourself** in the [General Discussion](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/general) category
   - Share your name and organization
   - Tell us about your place-based work
   - Let us know what brought you to ELEVATE

2. **Explore our categories**:
   - 💬 [General Discussion](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/general) - Open conversations
   - 📊 [Learning & Evaluation](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/learning-evaluation) - Research and methods
   - 📚 [Resource Library](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/resources) - Tools and templates
   - 🤝 [Collaboration Opportunities](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/collaboration) - Find partners
   - 📅 [Events & Webinars](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/events) - Community events
   - 🌟 [Success Stories](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/success-stories) - Share wins

3. **Read our [Community Guidelines](/guidelines)** to understand how we work together

4. **Complete your profile**
   - Add your organization affiliation
   - Share your areas of interest
   - Upload a profile photo

5. **Join the conversation**
   - Reply to topics that interest you
   - Ask questions in [Ask the Community](https://community.unitelearnelevate.com/c/ask-community)
   - Share your experiences and insights

### Quick Tips for Success

✅ **Be respectful and inclusive** - We welcome diverse perspectives and experiences

✅ **Stay on topic** - Keep discussions relevant to place-based philanthropy

✅ **Search before posting** - Someone may have already asked your question

✅ **Give more than you take** - Share your knowledge and experiences

✅ **Assume good intentions** - We're all here to learn and grow together

✅ **Cite your sources** - Give credit where it's due

✅ **Keep it professional** - This is a workspace for serious practitioners
