# About ELEVATE Commons

## Unite. Learn. ELEVATE

ELEVATE Commons is a dedicated community platform for place-based funders, particularly those focused on learning and evaluation, to share insights, exchange best practices, and co-create innovative solutions.

---

## Our Mission

**Transforming Place-Based Philanthropy** by elevating not just our initiatives but the very communities we serve.

Our country is a mosaic of different communities, each with its own story, strengths, and challenges. As funders working to learn, build evidence, and support these places, it's crucial that we understand the local context and target our investments to the things that residents say they want and need.

But we often confront a common hurdle: **the fragmentation of knowledge and resources**. How do we bridge this gap and harness our collective expertise to make change? How do we learn from past successes and foster trust with the communities we aim to serve?

ELEVATE is the answer to these questions.

---

## Why ELEVATE?

### Elevate Place. Elevate People. Elevate Action.

By joining the ELEVATE community, you'll be with like-minded learning funders pushing for transformational change. People who understand the nuances of your work and the many hats you must wear liaising with different stakeholder groups. Together, we'll elevate not just our initiatives but the very communities we serve.

### Streamline Collaboration, Enhance Effectiveness

Say goodbye to information overload. We curate and consolidate resources, freeing you to focus on what truly matters: catalyzing change.

### Increase Influence

As a collective voice, we wield greater influence in shaping the conversation about effective research, evaluation, and learning practices. Together, we champion strategies that resonate beyond boardrooms and into the communities we aim to serve.

## Our Core Principles

ELEVATE is rooted in four core principles:

### 1. Transformational, not transactional
We view data and evaluation not as compliance tasks, but as catalysts for transformative learning and growth. We're committed to using evidence and learning to drive real change, not just check boxes.

### 2. Community at the center
We prioritize community expertise and collaborate in ways that are inclusive, non-extractive, and that reduce the burden on grant partners. The voices and wisdom of the communities we serve guide our work.

### 3. Bringing our whole selves
We believe that the best work happens when people can bring their whole selves and have fun. We create space for authentic connection, creativity, and joy in our work.

### 4. Innovative progress
We push boundaries and advance place-based funding to make a real difference. We're not afraid to try new approaches, learn from failures, and continuously improve our practices.
